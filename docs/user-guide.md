## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Welcome

Welcome to the **Movie Ticket Booking Management Application** by CineWave Entertainment! This guide will help you navigate and use the application whether you're a customer booking tickets or a staff member managing bookings.

- **For Customers:** Browse movies, select seats, book tickets, and receive e-tickets

## 3. Customer Guide

### 3.1 Browsing Movies

**Step 1: Find a Movie**
- From the homepage, browse the **"Recommended Movies"** section
- Use the **search bar** to find specific movies by title, genre, or language
- Click on category tabs: **Movies, Stream, Events, Plays, Sports, Activities**

**Step 2: View Movie Details**
- Click on any movie card to view details
- See information about:
  - Movie description
  - Rating and reviews
  - Cast and crew
  - Duration and language
  - Age rating

### 3.2 Booking Tickets

#### Step 1: Select a Movie
1. Browse or search for a movie
2. Click on the movie you want to watch
3. Click **"Book Tickets"** button

#### Step 2: Choose Show
1. Select a date from the calendar
2. Choose a theatre from the list
3. Select a show time

#### Step 3: Select Seats
1. View the interactive seat map
2. Click on **available seats** (green color)
3. Selected seats will turn **red**
4. You can select up to **10 seats** per booking
5. View seat prices on the right sidebar

**Seat Legend:**
- 🟢 **Available** - Click to select
- 🔴 **Selected** - Your chosen seats
- ⚫ **Sold** - Already booked
- 🟡 **Bestseller** - Popular seats
- 🟠 **Reserved** - Temporarily held

#### Step 4: Add Food (Optional)
1. Scroll down to the **"Grab a Bite!"** section
2. Browse food categories: Combos, Popcorn, Snacks, Beverages
3. Click **"Add"** to add items to your cart
4. Adjust quantities using **+** and **−** buttons
5. Use code **MUNCH50** for 10% discount on snacks

#### Step 5: Review Booking
1. Review your selection in the **"Booking Summary"** sidebar
2. Check:
   - Movie, theatre, date, and time
   - Selected seats
   - Food items (if any)
   - Total amount
3. Apply coupon codes if you have one

#### Step 6: Enter Details
1. Enter your personal details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Mobile Number
2. Accept the terms and conditions
3. Click **"Proceed to Payment"**

#### Step 7: Confirm Booking
1. Review the booking summary
2. Click **"Confirm Booking"**
3. You have **15 minutes** to confirm your booking

### 3.3 Making Payment

1. Select a payment method:
   - **UPI** - Enter your UPI ID (e.g., name@bank)
   - **Credit Card** - Enter card number, expiry, CVV
   - **Debit Card** - Enter card number, expiry, CVV
   - **Net Banking** - Select your bank
   - **Wallet** - Select your wallet provider

2. Enter payment details
3. Click **"Pay Now"**
4. Wait for payment confirmation

### 3.4 Receiving Tickets

After successful payment, you will:
1. Receive an **email** with your e-ticket
2. Receive an **SMS** with booking confirmation
3. See the **Booking Confirmation** page with:
   - Booking reference number
   - Seat details
   - QR code for entry
   - Option to download PDF ticket

### 3.5 Managing Bookings

#### View Your Bookings
1. Click on your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **"Your Orders"**
3. View all your bookings

#### Cancel a Booking
1. Go to **"Your Orders"**
2. Find the booking you want to cancel
3. Click **"Cancel Booking"**
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Check refund eligibility and amount

**Cancellation Policy:**
| Time | Refund |
|------|--------|
| Within 1 hour of booking | 100% |
| 24+ hours before show | 80% |
| 12-24 hours before show | 50% |
| <12 hours before show | No refund |
| VIP customers | 90% refund |

### 3.6 Using the Search Feature

**Search Tips:**
- Type the first few letters of the movie title
- Use keywords like "action", "comedy", "Hindi"
- Search by actor name
- Search by director name

**Example Searches:**
- "Toxic" - Find movies with "Toxic"
- "Action" - Find action movies
- "Hindi" - Find Hindi language movies

## 4. Staff Guide

### 4.1 Accessing the Staff Dashboard

1. Sign in with your staff credentials
2. You will see the **Staff Dashboard**

### 4.2 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows:
- **Total Bookings** - All bookings today
- **Pending Bookings** - Awaiting review
- **Confirmed Bookings** - Approved bookings
- **Revenue Today** - Total revenue

### 4.3 Reviewing Bookings

#### Step 1: View Pending Bookings
1. Click on **"Pending Bookings"** on the dashboard
2. See a list of bookings awaiting review

#### Step 2: Review Booking Details
1. Click on a booking to view details
2. Check:
   - Customer information
   - Movie and show details
   - Selected seats
   - Payment status

#### Step 3: Approve or Reject
1. Click **"Approve"** to confirm the booking
2. Click **"Reject"** to reject the booking
3. Add staff notes if needed

### 4.4 Managing Shows

#### Create a New Show
1. Navigate to **"Show Management"**
2. Click **"Create Show"**
3. Fill in details:
   - Select Movie
   - Select Theatre
   - Select Screen
   - Date and Time
   - Ticket Price
4. Click **"Create Show"**

#### Edit a Show
1. Navigate to **"Show Management"**
2. Find the show you want to edit
3. Click **"Edit"**
4. Update details
5. Click **"Save"**

#### Cancel a Show
1. Navigate to **"Show Management"**
2. Find the show you want to cancel
3. Click **"Cancel"**
4. Confirm cancellation

### 4.5 Managing Seats

#### View Seat Map
1. Navigate to a show
2. Click **"View Seat Map"**
3. See all seats and their status

#### Mark Seats Unavailable
1. Click on a seat
2. Select **"Mark Unavailable"**
3. Add reason (optional)

#### Release Reserved Seats
1. Reserved seats are automatically released after 15 minutes
2. Manual release is also available from the seat map

### 4.6 Customer Communication

#### Send Email
1. Navigate to a booking
2. Click **"Send Email"**
3. Choose email template
4. Review and send

#### Send SMS
1. Navigate to a booking
2. Click **"Send SMS"**
3. Choose SMS template
4. Review and send

### 4.7 Reports

#### Generate Reports
1. Navigate to **"Reports"**
2. Select report type:
   - Daily Booking Report
   - Occupancy Report
   - Revenue Report
   - Popular Movies Report
3. Select date range
4. Click **"Generate"**

#### Export Reports
1. After generating the report
2. Click **"Export"**
3. Choose format: Excel, PDF, CSV

1. Navigate to **"Admin"** → **"User Management"**
2. Click **"Create User"**
3. Fill in details:

1. Navigate to **"User Management"**
2. Find the user
3. Click **"Edit"**
4. Change role
5. Click **"Save"**

### 5.2 System Configuration

#### Configure SLA Settings
1. Navigate to **"Admin"** → **"SLA Configuration"**
2. Adjust SLA times:
   - Customer Confirmation Time
   - Staff Review Time
   - Payment Processing Time
3. Click **"Save"**

1. Navigate to **"Admin"** → **"Pricing Configuration"**
2. Set base prices:
   - Classic Seat Price
   - Prime Seat Price
   - Recliner Seat Price
   - VIP Seat Price
3. Set surcharges:
   - Weekend Surcharge
   - Prime Time Surcharge
4. Click **"Save"**

### 5.3 Audit Logs

#### View Audit Logs
1. Navigate to **"Admin"** → **"Audit Logs"**
2. View all system actions
3. Filter by:
   - User
   - Date range
   - Action type
4. Export logs if needed

## 6. Troubleshooting

### 6.1 Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| **Cannot sign in** | Check email/password, reset password if needed |
| **Booking not confirmed** | Wait for email/SMS, check spam folder |
| **Seat selection not working** | Refresh page, try different browser |
| **Payment failing** | Try different payment method, contact support |
| **Not receiving emails** | Check spam folder, verify email address |
| **QR code not scanning** | Increase screen brightness, zoom in |

### 6.2 Error Messages

| Error | What it means | What to do |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| "Seats not available" | Seats were booked by someone else | Select different seats |
| "Booking expired" | 15-minute confirmation time passed | Create new booking |
| "Payment failed" | Payment was not successful | Try again or use different method |
| "Invalid coupon" | Coupon code is invalid or expired | Check coupon code |

## 7. FAQ

### Customer FAQs

**Q: How many seats can I book at once?**
A: You can book up to 10 seats per booking.

**Q: How long do I have to confirm my booking?**
A: You have 15 minutes to confirm your booking.

**Q: Can I cancel my booking?**
A: Yes, you can cancel from "Your Orders" section.

**Q: Will I get a refund on cancellation?**
A: Refund depends on when you cancel. See cancellation policy.

**Q: How do I get my e-ticket?**
A: You will receive it by email and SMS after payment.

**Q: Can I book food with my tickets?**
A: Yes, you can pre-order food during seat selection.

**Q: What payment methods are available?**
A: UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, Wallet.

**Q: How do I use a coupon code?**
A: Enter the code during the booking review step.

### Staff FAQs

**Q: How do I approve a booking?**
A: Go to Pending Bookings, review details, click Approve.

**Q: What happens if I don't review a booking?**
A: After 30 minutes, the booking escalates to supervisor.

**Q: How do I check seat availability?**
A: View the seat map from the show details.

**Q: Can I cancel a show?**
A: Yes, from the Show Management section.

**Q: How do I generate reports?**
A: Go to Reports, select type and date range, generate.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Booking** | A confirmed reservation for movie tickets |
| **Case** | A single booking instance from start to finish |
| **SLA** | Service Level Agreement - Time limits for actions |
| **E-Ticket** | Electronic ticket sent via email/SMS |
| **Show** | A specific screening of a movie at a specific time |
| **Seat Map** | Visual layout of all seats in a theatre |
| **Bestseller** | Popular seats that are in high demand |
